Loving God, Loving People

There's a place to serve and be served at every stage of life — from children through young adults, in prayer, healing, outreach and equipping.

◈

Catalyst City

Our local outreach ministry expands the kingship of Christ in the communities around us through love and discipleship, and by lending a helping hand.

◐

Children's Ministry

We partner with parents to lead children into a lifestyle of personal relationship with Jesus, helping them understand who God is and who God made us to be.

✚

Men's Ministry

We help the men explore and firmly establish our identities as sons of God. We want to build a community of true brothers through fun, food, fellowship and the Word.

✝

Ministry Team

We help release God's love, encouragement, empowerment and freedom by praying for individuals according to the leading of the Holy Spirit at worship services and other events. The intercessory team seeks God's will for the church in prayer.

❋

Renewal Ministry

Our inner healing ministry provides retreats where believers can personally encounter God's love and truth in the areas of their spiritual wounding. Through teaching, spiritual tools and prayer ministry, we equip believers to mature in their identity as God's children.

✎

Training School

We "equip the saints for the work of the ministry, for building up of the Body of Christ… to the measure of the stature of the fullness of Christ" (Ephesians 4:12–13). We resource the church in scriptural understanding, prophetic practices, inner healing, parenting, finances and more.

♀

Women's Ministry

We encourage the women to grow in their relationship with Christ, engaging them to be in fellowship with one another and empowering them to serve the church and community.

Join the Women's Group →
◔

Young Adults Ministry

We foster a community for unmarried believers to build friendships and share life together — meeting monthly for spiritual enrichment, deeper connections and outreach.

☆

Youth Ministry

We help middle and high school students make faith their very own, so they can live their fullest potential in Christ both inside and outside the church.
