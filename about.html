Who We Are

We're a Christian church in Bergen County, New Jersey. We invite you to any of our events and services to experience the presence of a loving and accepting God. Come as you are.

I'm Coming — Let Us Know
"But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth."
Acts 1:8

Catalyst's core verse commissions the church to depend on the powerful work of the Holy Spirit to break boundaries across four foundational pillars. Just as a table cannot properly serve its purpose if even one leg is weak or missing, we believe a forerunner needs to build strength in all four pillars.

Leadership

Meet Our Staff

Sam Lee

Senior Pastor

"I made a decision to follow Jesus Christ in high school, a decision for which I am thankful every day of my life. I went to Kenya, East Africa to do missions with the poor after seminary. While there, God burned in me the vision to train up disciples (forerunners) that will work together to break the boundaries in their own community and beyond. I am abundantly blessed with a house full of godly women: my partner and better half HeeJung and our four daughters. They are gifts from God reminding me daily of God's joy and kindness."

Katherine Park

Youth

"I came to Catalyst soon after experiencing the healing hand of the Lord and deciding to fully live for the One who gave me new, literal and spiritual life. What should have been a one-time visitation unexpectedly turned into God's providence. My desire is for God's children of all generations to encounter the tangible power of His love, so that there would be spiritual revival that starts at an individual level and spreads into our communities and throughout the world."

Julie Yu

Children

"Since coming to Catalyst in 2018, I have experienced healing and renewal that can only be explained by our supernatural God. God reawakened the dreams that He put in my heart long ago, dreams to serve the children and lead others in worship. I thank Him for the opportunity to serve alongside my husband, Josh, who shares my heart for ministry and is the best dad to our son, Luke. My passion is to see young hearts encounter the perfect love of the Father and testify to the saving work of Jesus in their own lives."
