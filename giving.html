Thanks and Purpose

We believe giving is an essential part of our devotion to the Lord in response to the grace and love of Christ in our lives.

To Honor the First Giver

By giving cheerfully, we are trusting God as our provider and the giver of every good and perfect gift.

To Be Part of God's Work

We want to devote our time, resources and effort on the very things Jesus gave His life for — the people, the true body of Jesus Christ. Would you partner with us in raising up regular people to become extraordinary disciples that will impact the world and beyond?
