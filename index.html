Our Vision

Forerunners Breaking Boundaries

We believe every good thing we carry starts with the love of Christ living in us. As His Spirit works in our hearts, we grow stronger in four ways — like a table needs every leg to stand, we need to grow in all four to walk fully in what God has for us.

Membership

Growing deeper in love for God and for one another.

Maturity

Being healed, formed, and shaped into the character of Christ.

Ministry

Discovering and living out the gifts God has placed in you.

Missions

Carrying God's love into our community and to the world.
